## List building and TAM

TAM means total addressable market: every company or person you could sell to.

- **JoJo.** A daily feed of companies whose public job postings describe your
  problem, with the quote as proof.
- **TAM Contact Harvester.** Named contacts for a market, free pages first.
- **PDF Contact Finder.** Finds contact-bearing PDFs and extracts every contact.
- **Domain & LinkedIn Finder.** Company names in, verified domains and LinkedIn URLs out.
- **TAM Research.** Finds and tests the registries that can list any company type.
- **Technology Finder.** Finds the customers of any technology.
- **Franchisee Finder.** A database of franchisee owners and multi-unit operators.
- **PE Acquisition Monitor.** Monthly private-equity acquisition lists with sources.
- **Acquisition Founder Sourcer.** Founders of acquired startups, ranked by how
  likely they are to be building again.

## Signals and monitoring

- **Competitor Monitor.** Watches company LinkedIn pages for the post types you define.
- **LinkedIn Engagement.** Everyone who reacted to or commented on a company's posts, enriched.
- **Video List Extractor.** Lists from screen recordings and screenshots.

- **Blueprint Playbook.** One company URL becomes a data-backed GTM playbook
  with pain segments, the public records behind them, and message drafts.
- **Find a Rep.** Sources sales reps by the selling context they worked in.
- **Rep Review.** Follows CRM leads to deals and gives each rep one coaching number.
- **Webinar Performance Report.** A webinar report from your CRM data.
- **Claude Deployment Report.** An AI-adoption report from usage exports and git history.
- **Clay Mobile Provider Benchmark.** Plans independent phone-data provider tests for your ICP.
- **Google Doc Builder.** Turns an analysis into a designed Google Doc.
- **Job Hunter.** An agent-run job search with evidence on every row.
- **Dual Brain.** Hands work to a second coding agent for a blind review.
- **Plain Plan Mode.** Makes Claude Code explain its plans in plain English.
