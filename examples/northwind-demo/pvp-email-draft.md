## The draft

**Subject:** Maplecrest Dental Group: one calendar before the next office opens

Hi {first name},

Your site says: "Cleanings, fillings, crowns and emergency care at five offices across the Springfield area."

When a practice runs several offices on separate calendars, the gap shows up the day
someone calls out sick. One operations director put it to us this way: "When a hygienist calls out sick, we lose a whole column of appointments and nobody at the other offices can see the gap."

Here is the checklist we would use to put every office on one calendar:

1. List every office's chairs and providers in one sheet, with each office's hours.
2. Pick one rule for same-day cancellations: who may offer the open chair, and to which patients.
3. Move one office first. Run the shared calendar there for two weeks before adding the next.
4. Before each new office goes live, check when its old scheduling contract renews and give notice in time.

No reply needed. If you want help with step 1, I can send a blank sheet.

{your name}
